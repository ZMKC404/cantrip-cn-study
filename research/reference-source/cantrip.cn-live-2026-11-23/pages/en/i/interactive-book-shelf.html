Interactive Book Shelf

Meng To's web bookshelf, where books have depth and paper edges. Pick one and it comes up close and opens; cover and background colors match, so each is its own picture.

By Meng To·Oct 21, 2026

Author's codeTry it live
Style
Editorial, Skeuomorphic
Tone
Colorful
Built with
Canvas, JavaScript

Design notes

The books get their physical feel from the cover, the thickness and the paper edges. Switching between the whole row on display and a close-up of a single book gives browsing the shelf a clear rhythm.

When a book turns its pages, you can see them lift layer by layer, while the serif title and the small text beside it stay aligned. The cover color echoes the background, so every book has a mood of its own.

Slingshot Sliders

gsimone's idea, played like Angry Birds: pull the slider back, and the landing spot and new value are drawn before you let go. It's fun, and you get to aim first.

Original by gsimone·Oct 18, 2026

Play full screen
Download HTML
Style
Playful, Technical
Tone
Dark
Built with
JavaScript, SVG, CSS

Design notes

  • Dragging becomes aiming: pull the opposite way, and the farther you pull, the farther it flies. The landing spot is drawn before you let go and the value is written in the title row first, so every shot can be aimed before you fire.
  • The band has a limit: the pull goes through a tanh, so it gets harder the farther you go and tops out at 95px. Your finger can travel far, but the slider always stays near the panel.
  • The landing spot comes from the product of the horizontal and vertical pull, and the arc height depends only on the vertical pull. So pulling straight down just hops in place, and pulling at an angle is what sends it far, as with a real slingshot.
  • Arrow keys, Page keys and Home/End adjust the value as usual, and Esc abandons the pull. A pull that’s too short doesn’t fire; the band wobbles the slider back to where it was.
